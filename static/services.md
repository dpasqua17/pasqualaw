# Practice Areas — Meghan Pasqua, LLC

Meghan Pasqua, LLC is a Dalton, Georgia law office serving families in Whitfield County, Murray County, and surrounding Northwest Georgia communities. All matters are handled under Georgia law. The office serves Spanish-speaking clients.

## Divorce

Advocacy through separation, filing, negotiation, and resolution.

## Child custody

Strategic representation focused on practical outcomes prioritizing children's well-being.

## Legitimation

Support for establishing parental rights and protecting parents' legal standing.

## Adoptions

Legal assistance for families through every step of the adoption process.

## Estate planning

Thoughtful counsel to help families prepare, protect, and plan for the future.

## Mediation

General civil mediation by attorney Meghan Pasqua, a Registered Neutral certified through the Georgia Office of Dispute Resolution. The firm's practice also includes probate and civil litigation matters.
